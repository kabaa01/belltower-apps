How Mapato Pack, Vukiq Kazi and Vukiq Card handle information

Last updated 4 November 2026. Issued under the Data Protection Act, 2019 (No. 24 of 2019) and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021. ODPC registration no. n/a.

1. Who is responsible

BellTower Enterprise Limited, P.O. Box 142 Village Market, Nairobi 00621, Kenya. Contact: belltowerkenya@gmail.com, +254 729 44 62 42. We are the data controller for the payment, licence and support information described in section 4. This notice is our notice to you under section 29 of the Act (duty to notify).

2. The short version

What you type into the apps, and the files you bring in, stay on your own device. We do not receive them and we cannot read them. We do not use advertising or analytics in the apps. This respects your right to privacy under Article 31 of the Constitution of Kenya.

3. What stays on your device

  • Mapato Pack: imported M-Pesa, bank and cash transactions (which may include names and phone numbers of other people), eTIMS invoice lists, client names, KRA PINs, settings and an activity log.
  • Vukiq Kazi: your name, handles, audience details, rates, income entries and tax notes.
  • Vukiq Card: the details on your card, which may include a child's name and a parent's phone number.
  • These are saved in your browser's local storage, not on our servers, and we do not use tracking cookies. If you clear your browser data, they are lost.
  • Backup files you download contain your records and are not encrypted. You are responsible for keeping them safe.

Because this information is processed on your device under your control and not by us, you decide what to enter and how long to keep it. If you are using the apps for other people's information, see section 9.

4. What we do receive and why

  • Payments: the details in the M-Pesa payment message, such as name, phone number, amount, code and time.
  • Licence details: the name you ask us to put on a licence. It is stored inside the licence code and shown on your reports.
  • Messages: what you send us by email, WhatsApp or phone.

We use these to confirm payment, issue your licence and eTIMS invoice, keep tax and accounting records, and answer your questions. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

5. Our lawful bases (section 30 of the Act)

  • Performance of a contract: to supply the licence you paid for.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation: to issue invoices and keep records under tax law, including the Tax Procedures Act, 2015.
  • Legitimate interest: to answer and keep a record of your support requests and to protect our licences from misuse.
  • Consent (as set out in section 32 of the Act) only where we ask for it, for example to send you updates. You can withdraw consent at any time.

6. How we apply the principles (section 25)

We collect only what we need, use it only for the purposes above, keep it accurate, keep it no longer than necessary (section 39), and protect it. Payment and invoice records are kept for at least five years because tax law requires it. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with your issue, then deleted unless the law requires us to keep them.

7. Third parties and transfers outside Kenya

  • GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may log technical data such as your IP address when a page loads.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs): only if you use PDF import in Mapato Pack, your browser loads a PDF-reading library from cdnjs, so your IP address is visible to that service. The PDF itself is read on your device and is not uploaded.
  • Safaricom (M-Pesa) handles your payment under its own terms and notices.
  • These providers may use servers outside Kenya. We do not send your records to them. Where we do transfer personal data outside Kenya, we will do so only as sections 48 and 49 of the Act allow.

8. Your rights (section 26) and how to use them

You have the right to be informed of the use of your personal data, to access it, to object to its processing, to have it corrected or deleted (section 40), and to receive a copy of what you gave us in a usable form. Some records, such as invoices, we must keep by law. Email us to make a request. We will reply as the Act and the Regulations require. For information that is only on your device, you can view, export or delete it yourself inside the app. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at www.odpc.go.ke.

9. Accountants and other professional users

If you use Mapato Pack for clients, you decide why and how their information is processed, so you are likely the data controller for it. You are responsible for having a lawful basis, telling your clients, registering with the ODPC where the Act and the Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021 require, and keeping the data secure. We do not receive your clients' information.

10. Security and breaches

Pages are delivered over HTTPS. Your records are only as safe as your device, so use a screen lock, keep your browser updated and delete your data on shared computers. If there is a personal data breach affecting information we hold, we will notify the Data Commissioner and affected people as section 43 of the Act requires.

11. Children

The apps are for adults. Vukiq Card can make a child safety card. Only a parent or guardian should enter a child's details, and the card should be kept safe. The Act has special rules for children's data, and we do not collect it from children.

12. Changes

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this notice and the date at the top.
